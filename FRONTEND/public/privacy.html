Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ConnectX ("we", "our", or "us"), created and maintained by Sudhanshu Ghosh. ConnectX is a real-time messaging, WebRTC calling, and collaborative communication platform accessible at https://connectx-chat-with.netlify.app. We are deeply committed to user privacy and minimal data retention.

2. Information We Collect

ConnectX collects only the essential data needed to establish real-time connections:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and optional avatar image.
  • Google Identity Information: When signing in through Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture to authenticate your session.

3. Zero-Database Architecture & Browser Storage

ConnectX operates on a Store-and-Forward ("WhatsApp Post Office") architecture:

  • Messages delivered to online recipients stream live peer-to-screen over WebSockets and are never stored permanently on our backend database.
  • Your chat history resides locally in your browser's private IndexedDB Vault on your device.
  • Undelivered messages to offline users are temporarily queued on the server with a 14-day auto-purge TTL and deleted instantly upon delivery.

4. Google Drive AppData Scope Usage

ConnectX offers an optional Bring-Your-Own-Drive (BYOD) cloud backup using the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata:

  • Purpose: We request this permission solely to upload and restore your personal chat vault backup in your private, hidden Google Drive application data folder.
  • Limited Access: ConnectX only touches its own connectx_chat_vault.json file. We cannot access, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your backup stays 100% private. We do not inspect, log, share, or sell your Google Drive content.

5. WebRTC Media Privacy

All audio, video, and live caption streams flow directly peer-to-peer between participating browsers via encrypted WebRTC. Media streams do not pass through or get recorded on our application servers.
